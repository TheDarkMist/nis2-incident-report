# National CSIRT, by jurisdiction

The national CSIRT of the client's jurisdiction is the recipient of the regulatory reports.
Address the report to the CSIRT of the country where the **regulated client** is
established, not where the agency is.

> **Verify before sending.** Each EU member state designates its CSIRT and reporting
> channel in its own NIS2 transposition, and several were still being finalised through
> 2025-2026. Treat the table below as a starting map, not as authoritative. Confirm the
> current designated recipient and the exact submission channel (often a single national
> portal) against the client's national law before any report goes out. If the
> jurisdiction is not listed, do not guess: ask.

| Jurisdiction | National CSIRT | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Italy | ACN / CSIRT Italia | Reporting via the ACN portal. |
| France | ANSSI / CERT-FR | |
| Poland | CSIRT NASK | Also CSIRT GOV and CSIRT MON for specific sectors. |
| Germany | BSI | |
| Spain | INCIBE-CERT / CCN-CERT | CCN-CERT for the public sector. |
| Netherlands | NCSC-NL | |
| Ireland | NCSC-IE | |
| Belgium | CCB / CERT.be | |

For any jurisdiction not in this table, identify the national CSIRT designated under that
country's NIS2 transposition, or tell the user you cannot confirm it and they should check.
